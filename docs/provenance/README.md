[../HISTORY.md](../HISTORY.md#glossary-of-provenance-names)). They are records, **not maintained**: their script
names, commands and paths refer to the old layouts ([MIGRATION.md](MIGRATION.md) maps them), and their claims are
superseded by [../findings.md](../findings.md). Where a record makes a claim that the logs do not support, a dated
correction note points to the findings; the text itself is otherwise verbatim.
