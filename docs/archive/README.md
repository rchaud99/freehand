# Archive

Documents carried over verbatim for provenance. They describe the two historical copies and are **not maintained**:
their script names, commands and paths refer to the old layouts (docs/MIGRATION.md maps them), and their claims are
superseded by docs/findings.md. Both old folders are read-only archives; this repository replaces them.

- `codex/`: the Codex publication-development copy (`freehand_publish`, 2026-09-28): README, PUBLICATION_PLAN,
  PUBLICATION_REPORT, CONTINUATION_REPORT, MODEL_STUDY, OVERNIGHT and KEY_FINDINGS ("evidence and claim
  boundaries").
  - `codex/publication/`: its four code-control study reports (`control_study`, `baseline_development`,
    `baseline_development_v2`, `baseline_holdout`) and the two figures they cite. The episodes behind them are
    imported under `results/legacy/codex/publication/`.
- `working_copy/`: the working copy's (`freehand_b`) KEY_FINDINGS ("key surprising findings", 2026-09-27) and the
  text of its 2026-09-27 three-model comparison (`three_model_comparison/results.md`, `findings.json`; the page and
  videos are in `results/media/`, not in git).
- `quote_bank/`: the 2026-09-27 curated highlights, as written. The maintained bank and highlights are in
  docs/quote_bank/.

The working copy's PREREGISTRATION, BENCHMARK_LADDER, ROADMAP and INTELLIGENCE_PERTURBATIONS are not here: they are
maintained verbatim, with provenance headers, as docs/preregistration.md, docs/benchmark_ladder.md, docs/roadmap.md
and docs/perturbations.md (part 2). Not copied at all: the Codex copy's review/evidence bundles (`release/*.zip`) and
the four full source snapshots under its `publication/*/source/`.
