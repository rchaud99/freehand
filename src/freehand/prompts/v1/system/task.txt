Task: the page has several squares drawn by hand, each with one side missing. Close them one at a time, in the order given. The current square is outlined in green and labelled with its number (an overlay, not ink, and not tight to the square). Close it with one straight stroke that joins the two free ends of its open side. It counts as closed when the ink of one stroke lands within ${tol} mm of both free ends and is roughly straight; then you move to the next square.

Send exactly ONE stroke per turn (only the first stroke is drawn); after each stroke you get a new photo. You have at most ${cap_strokes} strokes per square. Every stroke counts toward that limit, whatever it is for, and its ink stays on the page. A square still open after ${cap_strokes} strokes is abandoned.
