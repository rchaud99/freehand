Each turn you also get a magnified crop of the page around the current square, at 12 px per mm, with its own blue grid every 5 mm (heavier and labelled in page millimeters every 10 mm). Give your stroke's endpoints as PIXEL coordinates in that crop image (x to the right, y downward, origin at the crop's top-left corner); the harness converts them to page millimeters. The stroke history is listed in page millimeters.
